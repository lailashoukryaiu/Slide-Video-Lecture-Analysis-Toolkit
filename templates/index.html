      Slide detection options

      ? Help

      All choices keep detected full slide replacements. Variations include bullet points, text or diagrams appearing step by step on the same slide (sometimes called slide builds). Choose whether major variations or every addition should be saved as separate slide images. A slide must settle long enough to be sampled; brief flashes and continuous animation may not be detected.

      Advanced: method and limits
      Lower values detect more hard cuts. Higher values detect fewer cuts.
      Custom percentages combine additions appearing close together on the same slide. In adaptive mode, full slide replacements are preserved and earlier slide starts are not moved. Hard cuts use this as a minimum for all cuts.
      Slide limits

      A fixed limit applies at every detail level. Short clips may include up to 10 slides. At the limit, the most distinct changes are kept.

      ? Method help

      Adaptive mode ignores cursor and webcam movement and combines step-by-step additions according to the detail level.

      Saved slide sets

      ? History help

      View saved slides while new detection runs. Clicking a timestamp seeks the current video without replacing the active slide analysis. Sets are preserved from now on; previously overwritten slides cannot be recovered.

      Draw a box on the preview, choose an existing detected region, or scan for people. Detection samples every 0.5 seconds and can miss faces, moving people or webcam tiles. Review the entire output before sharing. Blur/pixelation are not guaranteed anonymization. Audio and subtitles are kept and may still identify people.
